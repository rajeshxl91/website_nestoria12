Frequently asked questions

How much do Nestoria services cost?

Browsing and consultations are free. Fees for buying, selling and management are agreed in writing before any work starts, so there are no surprises.

Are the listed properties verified?

Every listing is checked for ownership documents, accurate photos and correct details before it is published.

Can I rent and later buy the same property?

Some owners offer this option. Ask your advisor and we will tell you which listings allow it and on what terms.

Do you help first-time buyers?

Yes. We explain each step, from budgeting to closing costs, and can connect you with trusted lenders and inspectors.

How quickly can I schedule a viewing?

Most viewings can be arranged within two to three days of your request, and sometimes sooner for rentals.
