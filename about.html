We Help People Live Elevated

Nestoria is a real estate team built around one idea: finding a home should feel clear, fair and human.

Who we are

A real estate company that answers its phone

Nestoria brings together advisors, photographers, legal specialists and property managers under one roof. We list homes for rent and sale, but our real product is confidence: accurate listings, honest pricing and someone who stays with you until you have moved in.

Since 2014 we have helped more than 25,000 families find a place that fits their life and budget.

Our mission

To make every property decision simple and trustworthy by pairing verified information with real human guidance.

Our vision

A housing market where anyone can see the full picture, from price to paperwork, before they commit.

Our story

  1. 2014

    Two agents start Nestoria from a shared office with a single rule: never list a home we have not visited.

  2. 2018

    We add rentals and property management, expanding our advisory services.

  3. 2022

    Our verified-listing process launches, with photo, document and price checks on every property.

  4. 2026

    25,000 families later, we are expanding our advisory and investment services.
